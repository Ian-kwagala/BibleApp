LightWord Bible — Account & Cloud Data Deletion

In accordance with Google Play Store Data Safety requirements, LightWord Bible provides instant, self-service deletion of both your local app data and your optional Google Drive personal backup (user_bible_sync.json).

Option 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open the LightWord Bible app on your device.
  2. Tap the Versions (Cloud Backup) tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll to the Privacy, Legal & Data Safety section.
  4. Tap "Delete Cloud & Local Data" and confirm.

What gets deleted immediately: Your hidden user_bible_sync.json file inside Google Drive appDataFolder, all verse highlights, study notes, bookmarks, reading plan progress, XP points, and daily streaks (resetting all data to 0).

Option 2: Delete Directly From Your Google Account (Without the App)

Because LightWord Bible uses zero developer servers and stores your backup inside your own Google Drive hidden appDataFolder, you can also delete LightWord Bible's hidden data directly from Google Drive on the web:

  1. Go to drive.google.com and click the Settings gear icon → Settings.
  2. Select Manage Apps in the left sidebar.
  3. Locate LightWord Bible, click Options, and select Delete hidden app data or Disconnect from Drive.
